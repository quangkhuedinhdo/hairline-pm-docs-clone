What to expect

Results take time. Here's what the timeline looks like.

Every patient progresses differently — these are representative milestones, not guarantees.

1 Month

Early recovery phase

[Progress illustration — Month 1
Concept only, no identifiable photography]

Shedding is normal — and expected.

Transplanted hairs shed at 2–6 weeks. This is not hair loss; it is the follicles entering a resting phase before new growth begins.

Hairline Aftercare tracks symptoms daily and flags anything outside normal recovery range.
6 Months

Visible regrowth phase

[Progress illustration — Month 6
Concept only, no identifiable photography]

New growth is visible and thickening.

Most patients see significant new hair growth between months 3 and 6. Density continues to increase through month 12.

Your 3D scan at 6 months shows measurable follicle density changes vs. your pre-procedure baseline.
12 Months

Full result

[Progress illustration — Month 12
Concept only, no identifiable photography]

Full result achieved in most cases.

By 12 months, transplanted hair has reached its final density and texture. Your Hairline profile holds the full scan-based progress record.

Individual results vary. A Hairline-verified specialist can give you a personalised outcome estimate at consultation.

Common questions

Questions about the process.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is the assessment really free?

+
Yes, always. The Hairline app and its assessment are completely free to download and use. You only pay if and when you proceed with a treatment or consultation through a clinic.

Do I have to book a procedure to use Hairline?

+
No. Many users complete the assessment and use the clinic directory as a research tool without booking anything. There is no pressure to proceed. Consultations themselves are obligation-free.

How does Hairline vet its clinics?

+
Every clinic on Hairline passes a multi-step verification: accreditation documents reviewed, lead surgeon credentials confirmed, facility inspected (in person for Tier 1 partners), and patient reviews screened for authenticity. Clinics are re-verified annually. No clinic can pay to improve their ranking — match order is based entirely on outcome alignment with your profile.

Can I use Hairline if I live outside the UK?

+
Yes. Hairline is available worldwide. The provider directory covers clinics in 14 countries, and the app is available on iOS and Android in all major markets. If you're considering travelling for treatment, Hairline Travel can coordinate your entire trip.

What does Hairline Aftercare actually include?

+
Aftercare includes: daily symptom logging with smart alerts, 3D scan progress tracking at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months post-procedure, scheduled video follow-ups with your surgeon, and 24/7 access to a Hairline specialist. Learn more on the Aftercare page.

Is my scan and health data private?

+
Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored in GDPR-compliant infrastructure, and never sold to third parties. Your scan and assessment data is only shared with providers you explicitly choose to consult. You can export or delete your data at any time from within the app.

What if I want a second opinion?

+
Absolutely. Hairline is designed for this — you can share your profile and 3D scan with multiple matched providers and compare their treatment plans side by side before making any decision. There is no limit on consultations.
