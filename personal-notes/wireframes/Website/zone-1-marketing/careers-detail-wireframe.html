About the role

Hairline is connecting patients with the world's best hair restoration providers — and we need the engineering to match. As a Senior Backend Engineer on the Patient Platform team, you'll own the core APIs and data layer that power the patient journey: from the initial hair-loss assessment all the way through to post-procedure recovery tracking.

This isn't a "maintain the roadmap" role. We're at an inflection point — scaling from thousands to hundreds of thousands of active users — and you'll be making architectural decisions that will shape the platform for years. You'll work closely with our product and clinical teams to translate complex healthcare workflows into clean, performant APIs.

You'll be the third engineer on a backend team that ships fast and cares deeply about code quality. The stack is Node.js (TypeScript), PostgreSQL, and AWS, with a move toward event-driven architecture already underway.

What you'll do

  • Design and build the APIs that power patient onboarding, provider matching, and consultation booking — used by patients in 30+ countries.
  • Own the data models for patient health records, procedure history, and aftercare symptom logs — with HIPAA and GDPR compliance baked in from the start.
  • Lead the migration of our monolithic booking service to an event-driven microservices architecture using AWS SQS and EventBridge.
  • Write production-grade code (TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL) with the test coverage and documentation to match.
  • Collaborate with the mobile and frontend teams on API contracts — you'll be the backend voice in cross-functional design reviews.
  • Participate in an on-call rotation (low frequency — we invest heavily in reliability upfront).
  • Mentor more junior engineers and raise the quality bar across the team through code review and knowledge sharing.

What we're looking for

Must have
  • 5+ years of backend engineering experience, with at least 3 years in Node.js / TypeScript in production.
  • Deep familiarity with relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred) — schema design, query optimisation, migrations at scale.
  • Experience designing and versioning REST APIs for mobile and web clients.
  • A track record of working in regulated or sensitive data environments — healthcare, fintech, legal, or similar.
  • Strong opinions about reliability — you think about failure modes before you ship, not after.
  • Comfortable working asynchronously with a distributed remote team.
Nice to have
  • Experience building healthcare platforms with HIPAA / GDPR compliance requirements.
  • Familiarity with event-driven architecture patterns (SQS, SNS, EventBridge, Kafka).
  • GraphQL API experience.
  • Prior work at a marketplace or two-sided platform (matching, availability, booking systems).
  • Any professional experience in digital health, medtech, or life sciences.

What we offer

  • £90,000 – £120,000 base salary, benchmarked to the top 25th percentile.
  • 0.1% – 0.3% equity with a standard 4-year vest / 1-year cliff.
  • Remote-first. We have London and Dubai hubs, but remote is genuinely first-class — not a workaround.
  • £2,000/year learning and conference budget, with internal knowledge-share sessions.
  • Private health cover for you and your dependents from day one, including mental health support.
  • 28 days holiday + bank holidays + your birthday off. We actually track that people use it.
  • Hairline health benefit — free access to the full platform, including a funded consultation.
About Hairline

We're building the most trusted name in hair restoration.

Hairline connects people experiencing hair loss with vetted hair restoration providers worldwide. Our platform covers everything from the initial assessment and 3D head-scan diagnostics through to provider matching, treatment booking, and post-procedure recovery tracking via Hairline Aftercare. We also offer Hairline Travel — full medical-travel packages for patients who choose to be treated abroad. We're early, ambitious, and backed by investors who think this category is ripe for disruption.
