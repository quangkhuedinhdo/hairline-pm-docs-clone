Mission & values

What we believe in

We've built a set of values that are less about posters on the wall and more about how we make decisions every day.

🔬

Evidence, always

We don't make claims we can't back up. Every feature, every piece of content is grounded in clinical evidence.

🤝

Patient first

Commercial pressures are real, but they never override what's right for the patient. Full stop.

⚡

Move with purpose

We move fast, but we're deliberate. Speed without thought isn't ambition — it's noise.

🌍

Radically inclusive

Hair loss affects everyone. Our team, our product, and our providers should reflect that.

What we offer

Benefits built for real life

We want you to do the best work of your career. That starts with making sure you're looked after.

💰

Competitive compensation

Salary benchmarked to the top 25th percentile of your market, reviewed annually. Equity package included for all permanent hires.

🌐

Remote-first, genuinely

Work from wherever you do your best thinking. We have hubs in London and Dubai, but remote is a first-class option — not a compromise.

🏥

Private health cover

Comprehensive private health insurance for you and your dependents from day one. Mental health support included.

📚

Learning budget

£2,000 / year for courses, conferences, and books. Plus a structured mentorship programme and internal knowledge shares.

🏖️

Generous time off

28 days holiday plus bank holidays, plus an extra day on your birthday. We mean it — we track that people actually take it.

🧬

Hair health benefit

Every Hairline employee gets access to the full platform, including a funded consultation with one of our vetted providers.
