Section 5 · Article Body — editorial, section-by-section comparison

The Technique

Both procedures transplant follicular units — naturally occurring groups of 1–4 hairs — from a donor area (typically the back and sides of the scalp) to thinning or bald regions. The key difference is how those grafts are harvested. 1

FUE
Individual extraction

A 0.8–1mm circular punch tool removes each follicular unit one-by-one directly from the scalp. No strip of skin is removed, which means no linear scar. The technique demands more surgical time but leaves only faint dot scars that are virtually invisible with a short-cropped hairstyle.

FUT
Strip harvesting

A narrow horizontal strip of scalp (typically 1–2 cm wide) is removed from the donor area under local anaesthetic. Individual follicular units are then dissected from this strip under a microscope. The wound is closed with sutures, leaving a single linear scar that can be concealed with hair of medium length or longer.

"The best technique is the one your surgeon recommends for your specific hair characteristics — not the one with the most marketing behind it."

Scarring & Donor Site

Scarring is the most frequently cited reason patients choose FUE over FUT — but it's worth understanding the full picture. 2

💡 Key takeaway
  • ✓ FUE leaves hundreds of tiny dot scars — invisible unless the head is shaved very close.
  • ✓ FUT leaves one linear scar — visible if the hair is worn very short.
  • ✓ In skilled hands, both are well-tolerated and unlikely to draw attention.
  • ✓ Patients who regularly shave their heads to grade 1–2 should strongly consider FUE.

Recovery Time

Neither procedure requires a hospital stay, and both are performed under local anaesthetic in a single session. Recovery differences are modest but meaningful for patients planning around work or social commitments.

Results & Graft Survival

Long-term clinical outcomes between FUE and FUT are broadly comparable when performed by experienced surgeons. 3 FUT typically yields a higher total graft count in a single session — an important consideration for patients requiring large-scale coverage.

Cost Comparison

FUE is almost always priced higher than FUT because it is more labour-intensive — each follicle is extracted individually, which can take 4–8 hours for a mid-range session.

Who Is Each Right For?

FUE is better if you…
  • Want to wear hair very short (grade 1–3 clippers)
  • Have a smaller coverage area (Norwood 2–4)
  • Want a quicker return to physical activity
  • Are having a secondary or "touch-up" procedure
FUT is better if you…
  • Need maximum graft yield (Norwood 5–7)
  • Always wear hair medium length or longer
  • Want the most cost-effective large session
  • Have lower donor density (FUT preserves it better)

Common questions

FUE vs FUT: frequently asked questions

Is FUE more painful than FUT? ▼
Both procedures are performed under local anaesthetic, so you should feel minimal discomfort during the surgery itself. Post-operative soreness is generally mild with FUE; FUT patients may experience slightly more tenderness at the donor strip site for the first week.
Can FUT scars be repaired with FUE? ▼
Yes — this is a common revision strategy. FUE grafts can be transplanted directly into an existing FUT linear scar to break it up and make it less visible. The procedure is typically a small session (200–600 grafts).
Which lasts longer — FUE or FUT? ▼
Both procedures use permanent donor hair that is genetically resistant to DHT (the hormone that causes hair loss). Results from either technique are considered permanent, provided the donor area remains stable.
How do I know which Norwood stage I am? ▼
The Norwood scale classifies male-pattern hair loss from stage 1 (minimal hairline recession) to stage 7 (extensive baldness). The Hairline in-app assessment uses photos and pattern-recognition to estimate your stage and suggest appropriate treatments.
How much does a hair transplant cost in Istanbul vs the UK? ▼
Istanbul is the world's most popular destination for hair transplants, with prices typically 60–70% lower than the UK for equivalent graft counts. Hairline Travel bundles flights, hotel and transfers into a coordinated package — see our Istanbul clinic directory for verified options.
