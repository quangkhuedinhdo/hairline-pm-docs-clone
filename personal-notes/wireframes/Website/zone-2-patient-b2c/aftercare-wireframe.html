What is Hairline Aftercare

Recovery doesn't stop when you leave the clinic.

Most hair restoration patients are handed a printed leaflet and sent home. Hairline Aftercare replaces that with a structured, app-based recovery programme that begins the moment your procedure ends.

We monitor your recovery in three ways: through daily symptom logs you complete in under 60 seconds, through 3D scan comparisons at key milestones, and through direct access to a specialist team whenever you need reassurance.

Symptom logging

🎥

What to expect

Recovery is a process, not an event.

Hair transplant recovery unfolds over 12 months. Here's what Hairline Aftercare monitors at each stage — and what's normal.

✓
Days 1–7

Critical phase

The grafts are establishing. This is the most important week of your recovery.

  • Daily symptom log activated
  • Swelling peaks & resolves
  • Scabbing begins — do not touch
  • Sleep position monitored
  • Clinic alerted if anomalies flagged
1
Month 1

Shedding phase

Transplanted hair sheds. This is expected and does not indicate failure.

  • First 3D scan captured
  • Video follow-up with surgeon
  • Shedding explained & normalised
  • Minoxidil / Finasteride check-in
  • Donor area healing reviewed
6
Month 6

Visible growth

Significant new growth is visible. Most patients report a confidence shift at this stage.

  • 3D scan comparison (vs. Month 1)
  • Video follow-up review
  • Density assessment
  • Progress report to clinic
  • PRP or adjunct therapy review
12
Month 12

Final outcome

Full result is visible. The aftercare programme closes with a comprehensive outcome review.

  • Final 3D scan (full comparison set)
  • Outcome assessment with surgeon
  • Before-and-after gallery unlocked
  • Long-term maintenance plan
  • Aftercare programme completes

Recovery timelines are indicative and vary by individual, procedure type and clinic. Your Hairline Aftercare programme is personalised to your specific procedure and clinic's protocols.

Common questions

Aftercare, explained.

Everything you need to know about how Hairline Aftercare works, what's included, and how it connects to your clinic.

When does Hairline Aftercare start?

−
Hairline Aftercare activates on your procedure day. Your clinic registers your treatment in the platform before you leave, and you receive a setup prompt in the app within hours. Day 1 of your recovery log begins that evening.

Does it work with any clinic, or only Hairline-verified ones?

+
Full integration — shared dashboards, automatic alerts, and video follow-ups — requires your clinic to be on the Hairline platform. If you've already had a procedure at a non-verified clinic, you can still use the symptom logging and 3D scan features independently, though clinical alerts will route to our specialist team rather than your clinic.

Is the 24/7 access staffed by actual medical professionals?

+
The initial response is handled by trained aftercare specialists, not AI chatbots. If your query requires clinical judgement — for example, identifying whether a symptom pattern is medically significant — it's escalated to a hair restoration clinician within the same session. Urgent queries are prioritised and always reach a clinician.

How does the 3D scan work on a standard phone?

+
The Hairline app guides you through a standardised capture sequence using your phone's rear camera. You don't need a special device — the app stitches multiple photos taken from fixed angles into a 3D reference model. Scans taken at different milestones use the same reference points, so comparisons are consistent and accurate. iPhone 12 or later and most Android phones from 2021 onward are supported.

What happens if my symptom log flags something concerning?

+
If your log crosses a clinical threshold — for example, sustained redness, unexpected swelling, or reported pain above a certain level — the platform automatically notifies your treating clinic. You also receive an in-app prompt explaining what's been flagged and what steps to take. The clinic is required to respond within 4 hours under our partner agreement.

Is Hairline Aftercare included in my treatment cost?

+
This depends on the arrangement with your clinic. Many Hairline-partner clinics include the 12-month aftercare programme as part of their procedure package. Where it is not included, Hairline Aftercare is available as a standalone subscription. Pricing is shown in the app after you link your procedure.

How long does the aftercare programme run?

+
The standard programme runs for 12 months — which is the full recovery window for most hair transplant procedures. After Month 12, the programme closes with a final outcome review. Patients who want ongoing monitoring (for example, if continuing with Finasteride or Minoxidil) can extend the programme on a rolling basis.
