Hair restoration in London

London is home to one of Europe's most established hair transplant sectors, with a concentration of specialist clinics on Harley Street, in Marylebone, and across the City. Providers range from solo-surgeon practices to multi-discipline centres offering FUE, FUT, DHI, PRP, and Scalp Micropigmentation. Average procedure costs run higher than Istanbul or Doha, but you benefit from UK clinical regulation, proximity, and post-procedure access without international travel.

All clinics listed here are Hairline-verified: they have passed our credential checks, submitted to independent patient review, and agreed to transparent pricing standards. Listings are updated monthly.
