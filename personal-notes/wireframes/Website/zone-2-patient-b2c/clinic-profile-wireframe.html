About Vera Clinic SECTION 3

Vera Clinic is one of Istanbul's most established hair restoration centres, with over 12 years of clinical practice and more than 15,000 procedures performed. The clinic specialises in Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE), DHI, and combined hair-and-beard restoration for male and female patients from more than 60 countries.

The clinic operates three dedicated surgical theatres and a dedicated international patient coordination team, with full-service support from pre-arrival consultation through to post-procedure Aftercare. All consultations are conducted in-person or via the Hairline app by a board-certified trichologist before any surgical recommendation is made.

Vera Clinic is accredited by the Turkish Ministry of Health and the Joint Commission International (JCI). Patient outcome data is audited annually, and all before-and-after records are maintained with full consent documentation — accessible within the Hairline app under your treatment timeline.
