Pricing FAQ

Questions clinic owners ask us.

How is the commission calculated? ▾
Commission is charged as a percentage of the booked procedure value, only once the patient completes their consultation and confirms treatment — not on lead volume alone.
Is there a setup fee or minimum contract length? ▾
No. Starter and Growth plans are month-to-month with no setup fee. Annual commitment is optional and reduces your commission rate by 10%.
What happens if a lead doesn't convert? ▾
You're not charged. Commission only applies to procedures actually booked and confirmed through a Hairline-sourced lead.
Can I switch plans later? ▾
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at the start of any billing cycle from the Provider Portal, or by contacting your account manager on Enterprise.
How does Enterprise pricing work? ▾
Enterprise pricing is custom, based on location count, expected volume and integration needs. Book a call with sales for a tailored quote.
