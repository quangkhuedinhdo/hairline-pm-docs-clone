02 — Scheduling & Booking

Consultations that
book themselves.

Sync with the calendar your team already uses. Patients book directly against your real availability — no back-and-forth emails, no double bookings.

✓
Calendar management Two-way sync with Google Calendar, Outlook and Calendly. Block out time across multiple surgeons.
✓
Consultation booking Patients self-schedule against open slots; requests auto-confirm or route for manual approval.
