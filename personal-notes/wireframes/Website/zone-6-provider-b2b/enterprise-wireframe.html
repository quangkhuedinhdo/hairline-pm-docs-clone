Compliance & Security

Enterprise-grade,
by design.

Multi-site groups answer to boards, regulators and patients across jurisdictions. Hairline Enterprise is built to support that — with data residency controls, audit trails and compliance reporting built for scale.

GDPR Ready

Data residency, per jurisdiction

Each location's patient data is stored according to its own regional requirement — EU data on EU infrastructure, UK data in-country — even within a single group account.
