8 FAQ — schema-marked-up accordion, clinic-owner questions — Brief §5.6 §8 · §7.1: FAQ blocks schema-ready on all B2B pages
Frequently asked

Questions from
clinic owners.

How does the matching algorithm decide which clinic a patient sees? +
Patients are matched based on treatment type requested, geography (with travel preference factored in), budget range, and language preference. Clinics are ranked by verification status, patient rating, and response rate. You can set your own intake criteria in your clinic settings to filter out enquiries that don't fit your specialisation.
What does the onboarding process look like? +
After your demo, a Hairline account manager works with your team to complete the onboarding checklist: clinic verification, profile setup, surgeon credentials, treatment menu and pricing ranges. Most clinics are live within 5–7 business days.
Can I control which treatments or patient profiles I receive leads for? +
Yes. In your Provider Portal settings, you can specify which treatment types you accept, your preferred hair-loss stage range (Norwood I–VII), minimum and maximum budget brackets, patient travel capacity (local-only vs. medical travel), and language requirements.
What happens if a patient cancels or doesn't show up for their consultation? +
Hairline's automated reminder system (SMS + in-app notification) achieves a 62% reduction in no-shows vs. self-managed bookings. If a patient cancels within 24 hours, the lead is returned to your inbox for rescheduling. You are not charged for no-shows.
How do reviews and ratings work on the platform? +
Only patients who completed a procedure booked through Hairline can leave a review — verified-purchase only. Reviews are submitted via the Hairline Aftercare app. Your rating is calculated from treatment satisfaction, communication score, and outcome at 12 months (where available). All reviews are visible to you before publication; you have 7 days to flag any inaccuracies.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment period? +
No. The Starter and Growth tiers are month-to-month with no minimum commitment. Enterprise arrangements are typically annual, negotiated case-by-case. You can pause or close your account at any time with no penalty — all patient data is exportable on closure.
Does Hairline integrate with our existing clinic management software? +
Calendar sync (Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, Calendly) is available on all tiers. CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot) and EMR/EHR connections are available on Enterprise. A RESTful API is available for custom integrations — documentation available after sign-up.
