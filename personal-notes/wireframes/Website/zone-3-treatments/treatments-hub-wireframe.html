Decision guide

How to choose the right treatment.

The right treatment depends on four factors. Understanding where you stand across each will narrow your options significantly.

1

Your hair loss stage

Early-stage thinning responds well to medical treatments. Advanced loss (Norwood 5–7) typically requires a surgical option to restore coverage.

Stage I–III — medical treatments first
Stage IV–VII — consider transplant
Unsure — take the free assessment
2

Budget

Non-surgical options start from £10–£60 per month. Hair transplants are a single investment ranging from £2,500 to £18,000 depending on technique and graft count.

Under £100/month — Minoxidil, Finasteride
£500–£3,000 — PRP, SMP
£3,000+ — Hair transplant
3

Recovery tolerance

Medical treatments have no downtime. Hair transplants require 7–14 days of visible healing and 12–18 months for full results.

No downtime — Laser, Minoxidil, Finasteride
1–2 weeks — SMP, PRP
12–18 months — Hair transplant
4

Surgical vs. non-surgical

Only hair transplants are surgical. All other treatments are non-invasive and can often be combined — for example, Finasteride + PRP as post-transplant maintenance.

Non-surgical — all treatments except transplant
Surgical — FUE, FUT, DHI
Combination — transplant + ongoing medication
